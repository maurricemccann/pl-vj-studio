PSYCHED LUXURIES · Third Eye VJ Studio

Drag the white handles on canvas to reshape the selected ribbon's spline. Clicking a different ribbon's curve targets it for editing. Hover a segment to highlight it, click to select, wheel to add/remove points. Select a point: H for bezier handles, K to align, T to toggle tied/untied, W to reset its width handle. Drag the orange width handle to vary thickness at that point. F (or the button, top-right) toggles presentation/recording mode.
